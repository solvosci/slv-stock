Tracks INTECMAR's capture zones, product types and their
administrative status history, kept in sync automatically against the
official API.

Which of INTECMAR's own wordings (`Aberta`, `Pechada`...) are configurable, 
reviewable and editable from a dedicated list. A wording
never seen before is registered automatically the first time the sync
encounters it, blocking by default until an administrator reviews it,
so nothing slips through unblocked while unreviewed.
