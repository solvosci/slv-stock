Looks up an official sanitary guide (Documento de orixe) issued by Pesca
de Galicia's webservice and attaches it to a receipt line, filling in
the capture zone and biological zone straight from the guide's own data.

When a guide reports a capture or biological zone that has no real
INTECMAR status tracking behind it, this addon creates it and resolves
its state directly from the guide instead of leaving it undetermined.
INTECMAR's own data always takes precedence where it already exists;
this only fills genuine gaps.

A daily sync also pre-populates capture and biological zones from the
Xunta's own zone catalog, covering zones INTECMAR's feed does not carry.
If a guide is later corrected and a lot's own zone no longer matches it,
that lot is flagged for review instead of being rewritten automatically.

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Settings > Inventory > Sanitary Guide (Pesca de Galicia)* and
   set the webservice URL together with both sets of credentials: the
   HTTP Basic Auth pair required by the gateway, and the user sent
   inside the request body itself, identifying who is querying the
   document.
2. On a receipt subject to zone control, open *Operations > Detailed
   Operations* on the line and use the *Guía* button to look up a guide
   by its number. Confirming it fills in *Capture Zone* and *Biological
   Zone* on the line from the guide's own data.
3. Go to *Inventory > Products > Sanitary Guides > Guides* to see every
   guide looked up so far. *Query API* refetches a guide's data; *View
   PDF* downloads the official document on demand without storing it.
4. A guide's *Lots* and *To Review* buttons open the lots linked to it;
   *To Review* only lists the ones whose own zone no longer matches the
   guide's current data.
5. A scheduled action syncs the Xunta's zone catalog daily, creating any
   capture or biological zone it lists that INTECMAR's own sync does not
   yet cover.
