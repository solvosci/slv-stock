Blocks lots captured from a capture zone/product-type whose INTECMAR
administrative status is currently closed, tracking each lot's capture
origin(s) - capture zone, product type and date - against
``stock_intecmar_capture_zone``'s status history.

A blocked lot is physically moved to the same quarantine area used by
``stock_lot_quarantine``'s purification hold, and stays there until its
capture zone/type is reopened for its capture date - reflected
automatically whenever the status history changes - or a manager
releases it by hand. Unlike a purification hold, it is never lifted
automatically just because time has passed.

Usage
=====

1. On each product subject to toxin control: *Inventory > Products >
   Products*, set its *INTECMAR Product Types*.
2. When receiving such a product, pick the *Capture Zone* on the receipt
   line before validating. A line received without one is not
   toxin-controlled, and a note is posted on the picking instead.
3. Go to *Inventory > Products > Toxin Control > Toxin Block - Pending
   Review* to see every lot currently blocked.
4. A lot is released on its own once its capture zone/type is open again
   for its capture date, reflected the moment that period is recorded in
   ``stock_intecmar_capture_zone``. A Purification manager can also
   release it directly from that same list with the *Release* button,
   regardless of what the zone data says.
5. A lot's own form shows its capture origins and their current INTECMAR
   status on the *Toxin Zone Control* page.
